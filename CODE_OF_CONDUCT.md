# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and direct;
- focus feedback on the work and its impact;
- assume good intent while still being precise about problems;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- respect privacy and do not share private transcripts, screenshots, credentials, or local user data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior is abusive, unsafe, or disruptive to the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Include links or screenshots where relevant, and avoid escalating public conflict.
